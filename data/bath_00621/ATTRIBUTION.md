# Public CAD-edit data attribution

Gopsill, J., Snider, C., and Hicks, B. (2019). Dataset for “The emergent structures within digital engineering work: what can we learn from dynamic DSMs of near-identical systems design projects?” University of Bath Research Data Archive. DOI: https://doi.org/10.15125/BATH-00621 . Accessed 2026-10-28 at https://researchdata.bath.ac.uk/621/ .

The database and source README have Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licensing: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The demonstration notebook is offered under the MIT License on the archive record. It is retained as documentation and has not been executed. The source creators are independent data authors, not the submission authors; retain their attribution in review copies.

Derived graphs are our transformations: >=5 distinct edit times per file, one/twelve-hour co-edit frequencies, symmetrization, pruning at 1/4, and integer quantization. See provenance_v5.json and scripts/import_bath_graphs.py. All qualifying vertices, including isolates, remain. These are association proxies, not verified physical or causal interfaces; we adapt rather than fully reproduce the original DSM pipeline.

A/B/C identify three historical projects, NOT our binary responsibility pools. Ownership, team qualification, reserved capacity/slack, costs, perturbations and handoff budgets are synthetic assumptions. No observed transfer decision or staffing benefit is claimed.
